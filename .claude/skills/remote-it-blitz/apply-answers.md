## Per-application tailoring (the one thing worth 30 seconds)

Even on one-click, mirror **one exact keyword** from the posting into the profile headline
or the free-text box — ATS keyword matching rewards the posting's literal term. Examples:
if it says "service desk", don't only say "help desk"; if it says "ITIL", and it's true,
say ITIL.
